# David: Shepherd of Israel — Game Design

Seven-stage Bible-inspired action platformer. David faces wildlife and fictionalized hostile threats before the final encounter with Goliath. The first level introduces sling attacks, movement, stones, and the Alpha Wolf boss. Later levels and six other bosses require narrative and gameplay design review. Biblical events should be distinguished from invented gameplay encounters.

## Core mechanics

Move, double jump, charge and fire sling, collect ammunition, earn upgrades, activate checkpoints, defeat bosses. Smooth stones are standard; heavy stones deal bonus damage but are limited.

## Stage 1

Pasturelands: wolves, elevated routes, healing pickups, heavy stones, Alpha Wolf boss.

## Future

Design seven unique environments, bosses, enemy behaviors, sling upgrades, audio, save progress, accessibility and responsive touch input.
