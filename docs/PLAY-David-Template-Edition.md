# David: Shepherd of Israel — template edition

This is the template-focused exploration prototype. The original kit’s animated character, enemies and final guardian remain placeholders while the David story and search progression are adapted.

## Play

Open `David-Template-Edition-v0.4.1/David-Shepherd-of-Israel.exe` when the Windows build is present beside this guide. Keep the executable with its adjacent Data folder and Unity files.

In Unity, the active project is `GameKitFoundation`. Open `Assets/David/Scenes/Start.unity` and press Play. Choose **BEGIN**.

## Controls

- A / D: move
- Space: jump
- S: crouch; S + Space: drop through a platform
- W / S: look up / down
- K: staff attack after the kit’s weapon pickup
- O: ranged attack after the kit’s weapon pickup
- E: use a marked passage
- Escape: native pause/options menu

## Search route

1. **The Shepherd’s Watch:** follow the authored pasture route and read the existing trail posts.
2. **The Lion’s Ravine:** the central area connects several searches. Find its hidden trail key and explore the side passages.
3. **The Bear’s Hollow:** search the upper route for another key. The kit’s weapon discovery and breakable-wall puzzle remain intact. Return to the ravine.
4. **The Unexpected Calling:** descend the cistern route for the third key and return. These three keys unlock the central passage.
5. **Bread for the Brothers:** search the reused ravine terrain for three supply markers; return to its central passage.
6. **Five Smooth Stones:** search the pasture ledges for five stone markers. The native HUD displays five inventory symbols; the right-hand exit requires all five.
7. **The Valley of Elah:** the kit’s original final arena and guardian remain in place for combat and puzzle testing.

The glowing collectible represents trail keys, supply markers or stone markers according to the chapter. Its existing artwork is intentionally reused to preserve a consistent style.

This pass retains the original menus, dialogue presentation, health/key HUD, switches, doors, checkpoints, enemies, effects and audio. It removes the mismatched generated outfit, props, custom enemies and extra text overlays. No additional Store packs were imported.

The character portrait and guardian are still the kit’s originals. A finished David costume, biblical enemy presentation and full narrative polish remain future work; this prototype should not be treated as the completed seven-level game.
