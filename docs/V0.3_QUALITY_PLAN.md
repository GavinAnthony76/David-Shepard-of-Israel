# v0.3 — Visual and Combat Quality Gate

## Objective
Replace prototype readability with an original, cohesive shepherd-era pixel-art identity while keeping v0.2's responsive movement and combat intact. Do not add additional levels until Level 1 passes this gate.

## Art direction
- **Palette:** dawn blues, limestone gold, olive green, warm ochre, clay red. Restrained saturation; high-contrast interactive objects.
- **David:** readable shepherd silhouette, tunic, belt, satchel, staff/sling at rest; facing, run, idle, ascent, apex, descent, charge, release, hurt and landing states.
- **Wolves:** quadruped silhouettes, ear and tail animation, stalking, wind-up, leap, recoil, death.
- **Alpha Wolf:** 2–3× standard wolf silhouette, visible scars, distinct tell colors and posture. Avoid simply scaling a rectangle.
- **Terrain:** foreground grass, eroded limestone platforms, root-covered ledges, bushes, stone outcrops, parallax hills and distant clouds. Ensure collisions match visible edges.
- **VFX:** sling orbit, projectile streak, charged glow, hit sparks, dust, enemy recoil, restrained camera shake; accessibility toggle for flashes/shake.
- **Originality:** no Rygar sprites, maps, music, or extracted assets.

## Level 1 layout
1. **Pasture introduction (0–20%):** movement and first wolf, low-risk double-jump introduction.
2. **Broken terraces (20–45%):** alternating heights and recoverable drops; optional heavy-stone cache.
3. **Ravine approach (45–65%):** multi-platform jumps, enemy placement that rewards positioning; safe checkpoint.
4. **Wolf den (65–85%):** narrower spaces, mixed enemy pressure and sling angles.
5. **Alpha Wolf arena (85–100%):** ample telegraph space, no cheap spawn collisions, distinct entrance and victory transition.

## Combat requirements
- Press/release sling has immediate, consistent timing; charged attacks must not lock player movement.
- Separate attack startup, active, recovery; no accidental queued shots.
- Distinct heavy-stone damage, speed and arc with limited supply; basic stones remain unlimited.
- Knockback and invulnerability frames, with clear on-screen feedback.
- Enemy AI must not damage on every overlap frame or teleport to player.
- Boss phase A: stalking + lunging; phase B (<50% HP): faster pattern + leap. Each attack has telegraph, active and recovery windows.
- Boss defeat disables hitboxes, plays death/victory state and persists stage completion.

## Mobile-first quality
- Dedicated left/right movement buttons, jump and sling hold/release buttons; multitouch supported.
- Controls must remain visible in landscape and not overlap the combat HUD.
- Scaled rendering and device-pixel-ratio handling; 16:9 logical view without cropped gameplay.
- Pointer cancellation and window blur release held actions.

## Engineering quality gates
- Fixed timestep simulation (e.g. 60Hz) with render interpolation.
- Unit tests: double-jump reset, one-way platform landings, projectile collision, boss phase transitions, touch pointer tracking.
- CI: install, test, build; automated browser smoke test where available.
- Performance target: 60 FPS on representative desktop and midrange phone; no console errors.
- Acceptance: user can complete Level 1 without unclear hitboxes or forced blind jumps.

## Order of implementation
1. Decouple update, rendering, input and level data from src/main.js.
2. Implement collision/physics regression tests and mobile touch controller.
3. Add original sprite assets/animations and scenery layers.
4. Build authored Stage 1 terrain and checkpoints.
5. Replace Alpha Wolf with state-machine boss and telegraphed attacks.
6. Tune combat, sound, HUD, accessibility and performance.
7. Publish preview; user playtest; only then merge.
